Low-cost real-time fabric colour variation detection

A consumer USB webcam, daylight-balanced LED panels and open-source software, instead of a spectrophotometer.

The research system

Python desktop application with a Logitech C270 USB webcam and a 5500 K LED booth. All research measurements are taken with this system.

  • Colour difference in CIEDE2000, with ΔE*ab and CMC(2:1) recorded
  • Five readiness checks before a test: camera, exposure, stability and even lighting
  • Pass/fail limit derived from the system's own measured noise
  • Reports with per-frame CSV export for analysis
